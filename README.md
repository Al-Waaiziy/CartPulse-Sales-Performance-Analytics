## Project Overview

**CartPulse Sales & Performance Analytics** is an interactive business intelligence dashboard developed to analyze sales performance across products, regions, payment methods, customer demographics, discounts, and shipping operations.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key commercial metrics and allows users to explore sales patterns through interactive filters and visualizations.

## Project Objectives

The analysis focuses on understanding:

- Overall sales and order performance
- Revenue by product category
- Product demand
- Regional sales performance
- Payment method preferences
- Customer distribution by gender
- Shipping costs
- Discount patterns
- The relationship between discounts and profit margin
- Sales trends over time

## Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard highlights:

- **34.5K** Total Orders
- **5.87M** Total Revenue
- **51K** Total Items Sold
- **212.25K** Total Shipping Cost

## Dashboard Analysis

### Revenue by Category

Product categories were analyzed to identify the categories contributing the most revenue.

**Electronics** generated the highest revenue at approximately **3.3M**, followed by Home and Sports.

Other categories included:

- Fashion
- Beauty
- Toys
- Grocery

### Regional Analysis

Sales and discount activity were examined across:

- Central
- East
- North
- South
- West

The dashboard includes both regional comparisons and a geographic visualization to provide a broader view of sales distribution.

### Payment Method Analysis

Customer payment preferences were analyzed across:

- Credit Card
- Debit Card
- Cash on Delivery
- UPI
- PayPal
- Wallet

Credit Card recorded the highest transaction value among the payment methods displayed in the dashboard.

### Customer Gender Analysis

Revenue was analyzed across:

- Female
- Male
- Other

The dashboard provides a visual comparison of revenue contribution by gender.

### Discount Analysis

The project examines discount distribution across regions and explores the relationship between discount activity and profit margin.

The **Discount Impact on Profit Margin** visualization provides a time-based view of changes in discount and profit-margin metrics.

### Shipping Analysis

Total shipping cost is included as a key performance indicator, allowing shipping expenditure to be considered alongside revenue, orders, and product sales.

## Interactive Features

The dashboard includes interactive filters for:

- Region
- Category
- Payment Method
- Date Range

These filters allow users to explore specific segments of the sales data and examine changes in performance across different business dimensions.

## Key Observations

The dashboard highlights several patterns within the dataset:

- Total revenue reached approximately **5.87M**.
- Electronics generated the highest category revenue.
- Credit Card was the most prominent payment method shown.
- Sales activity is distributed across five regions.
- The dashboard provides a comparison of revenue contribution by gender.
- Discounts and profit-margin trends can be examined over time.
- Shipping cost represents an additional operational metric alongside sales performance.

## Dashboard Design

The dashboard combines:

- KPI cards
- Bar charts
- Donut charts
- Treemap visualization
- Trend analysis
- Geographic mapping
- Interactive slicers
- Date filtering
- Comparative analysis

The layout was designed to provide a single-page view of the main sales and performance indicators while allowing deeper exploration through interactive filters.
